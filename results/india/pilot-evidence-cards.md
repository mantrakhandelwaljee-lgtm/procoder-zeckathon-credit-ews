# Indian evidence pilot

**These are source-reviewed financial examples, not a validated historical watch-list.**
Historical publication dates remain unverified. No four- or eight-quarter recovery claim is made.
Scores are transferred from the Polish benchmark and are not calibrated Indian default probabilities.
A four-company universe has floor(4 x 5%) = 0 formal alert slots. All four are shown for evidence review only.

Transferred risk score: **0.1034**. Altman Z'' screen: **0.617**.
Source: [original annual report](https://www.bseindia.com/bseplus/AnnualReport/533177/5331770316.pdf); field-level PDF pages are in `pilot_field_sources.csv`.

- **Inventory days relative to sales = 3.8405**; contribution +0.0169. This observed value raises the model score relative to the Polish background. Check stock realization, obsolescence and sector-specific inventory needs.
