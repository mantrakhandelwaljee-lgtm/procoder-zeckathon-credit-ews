29 October 2026. **The dossier is ready for review; the case is not yet approved for the primary Indian backtest.** The frozen model ranks Bhushan Steel highest in a four-company FY2012 numerical comparison. That finding depends on disclosed accounting mappings and an assumed report-availability bound. It is not a demonstrated four-quarter recovery.

**Updated reconciliation:** see [payment records and original FY2011 verification](BHUSHAN_RECONCILIATION.md). Contemporary indexed trustee records now corroborate the December 2013 payment failure and later cure reporting. Original FY2011 numbers for all four issuers are separately curated; current-classification and public-timestamp gates remain unresolved.

## What this work establishes

The extension adds 21 downloaded primary-source PDFs, a separate manifest with hashes, nine reviewed numerical examples, field-level provenance, three soft-signal families, actual-model explanations and 24 issuer/cutoff checks. Four failed routes are retained as retrieval exceptions. Downloaded reports span FY2010–FY2013 for Bhushan Steel, JSW Steel, Tata Steel and SAIL. Download coverage is not financial-review coverage.

The numerical panel comprises each issuer's FY2012 statement and its FY2011 comparative **from that FY2012 source**, plus Bhushan's original FY2013 statement. The FY2011 comparative versions are never represented as information published in FY2011. Nine balance sheets reconcile within rounding after separately reported equity items are accounted for. A single researcher reviewed source text and rendered statement/note pages; this is not an independent second review.

**Endpoint decision: earliest documented failure is 31 December 2013; first-default completeness remains unresolved.** Neither August 2014, July 2015 nor CIRP is promoted to `verified_first_default`. ## Accounting decisions and numerical result

Use consolidated statements consistently for this comparison. Convert Bhushan's INR lakh values to INR crore; peers report crore. Use net revenue after excise, current trade receivables, parent-attributable reported profit, and an EBIT proxy of PBT after exceptional items plus the P&L finance-cost charge. Reported extraordinary/nonrecurring effects remain visible, not silently normalized.

Total liabilities are the sum of current and non-current liabilities. Minority interests, preference application money, separately presented perpetual securities and other equity-like items are not manufactured into liabilities by subtracting only common equity from assets. X8 still uses each report's parent shareholders-funds line; Tata's separately presented hybrid securities are outside that numerator. This convention needs approval and sensitivity review.

Retained earnings is a **core earned-reserve proxy**: P&L surplus plus general reserve plus debenture/bond-redemption reserve. It excludes share premium, capital reserves and OCI. Smaller other reserves and acquisition adjustments still need origin review. It is not certified as the full retained-earnings definition required by the benchmark. These mapping qualifications are attached to every input and score.

The fixed Altman proxy ranks JSW as more distressed than Bhushan, illustrating model disagreement rather than a reason to choose a favorable measure after observing outcomes.

Bhushan's three largest positive model contributions are quick-ratio weakness (0.295), low sales/assets (0.295) and limited book equity/liabilities (0.295). The explanation uses permutation SHAP of the actual calibrated ensemble with a development-only background; all contributions plus the baseline reproduce the displayed score. Full values, contributions and provenance are saved in the linked outputs.

The FY2011 comparative and FY2013 exploratory Bhushan scores are 0.081082 and 0.052285. Do not present this decline as a validated historical signal trajectory: publication versions and mappings remain unresolved.

Four issuers yield `floor(0.05 × 4) = 0` formal review slots. No formal alerts, recovery rate or portfolio cost are reported. The development-selected numerical threshold is not an approved Indian policy. The three peers remain provisional: Tata's global operations, SAIL's state ownership and the issuers' different sizes/business mixes require matching and outcome review.

M-Score and F-Score remain incomplete. COGS, SG&A, issuance and earlier beginning-asset definitions must be verified rather than inferred from broad expense totals or unchanged paid-up capital. Partial F flags and missing-field lists are saved; no partial sum is labeled a complete F-Score.

## Soft evidence already visible in the FY2012 report

These are investigation prompts, not fitted features or findings of fraud. Their numerical/text content was reviewed on rendered pages; historical use still depends on the publication gate above.

- **Related-party transactions, PDF page 114:** disclosed goods/services purchases total INR699.9274 crore versus INR478.2330 crore in the comparative column. Review counterparties, pricing and settlement. Ordinary related-party purchases alone do not establish misconduct.
- **Subsidiary audit/support disclosure, PDF page 113:** note 36 reproduces Bowen Energy's going-concern dependence, negative equity and parent support. Parent management explains exploration losses capitalised in group CWIP. This is a subsidiary disclosure, not a qualified parent audit opinion. Review asset recoverability and support obligations.
- **Capitalised finance costs, PDF page 115:** note 40 includes INR1,675.1386 crore during construction/trial run, versus INR1,186.0581 crore previously. This is separate from the INR1,046.3781 crore P&L finance charge. Review cash debt service and project completion risk; do not subtract capitalised costs twice or label permitted capitalization as manipulation.

[Original FY2012 report](https://www.bseindia.com/bseplus/AnnualReport/500055/5000550312.pdf). Later 2014 rating watch and arrest-related context are too late for these four-/eight-quarter scenarios and are not used to improve their scores.
